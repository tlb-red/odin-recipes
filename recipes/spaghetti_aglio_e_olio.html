Spaghetti aglio e olio



Originating from the peasant traditions of Southern Italy—specifically the regions of Campania and Abruzzo—this dish was historically known as “the poor man’s pasta” because it required only the humblest pantry staples.


    Ingredients:

  • Spaghetti
  • Olive oil
  • Sliced garlic
  • Salt
  • Crushed red pepper flakes

  1. Boild water for the pasta
  2. Heat a large skillet with olive oil
  3. Once the water boils, add salt and spaghetti
  4. A few minutes after you have the pasta boiling, add the garlic slices to the heated oil.
  5. Sauté the garlic until it is a nice light golden brown (should take 3 to 5 minutes)
  6. Add in the hot pepper flakes and stir to combine with the garlic and oil
  7. Once the garlic reaches golden color, remove the pan from the heat while the pasta keeps cooking.
  8. Cook the pasta until it's just before al dente (set a timer for 1 minute less than the cooking time on the pasta instructions)
  9. Reserve half a cup of starchy pasta water
  10. Add the pan back onto medium-high heat. Drain the pasta and immediatelly add to the pan with the garlic and oil
  11. Add in the reserved pasta water and toss to combine
